Professional Property Management
in Abu Dhabi

01 — Tenant Communication

We provide a professional point of coordination for tenancy-related communication, helping reduce the amount of day-to-day involvement required from the property owner.

02 — Lease Administration

We help maintain an organized approach to tenancy documentation and lease-related requirements throughout the rental period.

03 — Rental Follow-Up

We support owners with rental payment follow-up and related communication where required.

04 — Renewal Coordination

As a tenancy approaches expiry, timely communication becomes important. We help coordinate renewal-related requirements between the landlord and tenant.

05 — Property-Related Coordination

Where property-related issues require attention, we help coordinate communication and the appropriate next steps within the agreed management scope.

06 — Move-In Coordination

A well-organized tenancy should begin smoothly. Primeval assists with the coordination required during the tenant's move-in process.

07 — Move-Out Coordination

When a tenancy ends, we help coordinate the relevant move-out requirements and communication.

08 — Documentation Management

Property and tenancy documentation can quickly become difficult to manage. We help maintain an organized approach to documentation relating to the managed property.
